# Catalogue ownership seams

Choose the route owner before changing an authored page, stylesheet, or browser assertion. Imports across these boundaries are intentional: a consumer can use a specimen or card without owning its implementation.

- **Discovery** owns [`components/explorer-state.ts`](../../catalogue/pages/components/explorer-state.ts), directory/collection rendering, local [`navigation.tsx`](../../catalogue/pages/components/navigation.tsx), and [`detail-navigation.tsx`](../../catalogue/pages/components/detail-navigation.tsx). The latter exposes `ComponentDetailBreadcrumb({ entry })` and `ComponentDetailNavigation({ entry, state })`; return and neighbour travel can evolve without editing the detail specimen. Their route-specific treatment lives in [`component-discovery.css`](../../catalogue/styles/component-discovery.css).
- **Detail** owns [`detail-state.ts`](../../catalogue/pages/components/detail-state.ts), [`detail-page.tsx`](../../catalogue/pages/components/detail-page.tsx), and [`component-preview.tsx`](../../catalogue/pages/components/component-preview.tsx), styled by [`component-detail.css`](../../catalogue/styles/component-detail.css). `ComponentSpecimen` and `ComponentSurfaceControl` are shared call boundaries for Compare and conformance consumers. A new detail control belongs here; a consumer-specific arrangement belongs to that consumer.
- **Compare** owns [`pages/compare/`](../../catalogue/pages/compare/) and [`compare.css`](../../catalogue/styles/compare.css), including its control labels and group spacing. It composes the detail specimen instead of copying it.
- **Terminal** owns [`cli-preview.tsx`](../../catalogue/cli-preview.tsx) and [`cli-preview.css`](../../catalogue/styles/cli-preview.css), shared by browser routes that display terminal output. Terminal labs and their local responsive layout remain in [`terminal.css`](../../catalogue/styles/terminal.css).
- **Shell** owns [`pages/shared.tsx`](../../catalogue/pages/shared.tsx), [`shared.css`](../../catalogue/styles/shared.css), and page-scale [`responsive.css`](../../catalogue/styles/responsive.css). Generic `CatalogueIndexCard`, result headers, breadcrumbs, empty states, and copy rows serve multiple routes. A route adds a scoped local modifier to these authorities; it does not fork the shared card.
- **Glyphs and compositions** retain their own page trees, styles, and browser runners. [`components.css`](../../catalogue/styles/components.css) contains the canonical example-layout harness, not route chrome. The image-capture document consumes that harness directly; changes to it require the image command to refresh provenance.

Route-responsive rules travel with their owner's stylesheet. [`catalogue.css`](../../catalogue/catalogue.css) is the serialized import boundary; preserve base-before-override ordering when enrolling a sheet.

## Check ownership

The [`browser-check-plan.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/browser-check-plan.ts) plan and [`scripts/conformance.ts`](../../scripts/conformance.ts) remain the shared enrollment points. Every registered family still needs exactly one family check, and every declared check needs an executable runner.

The Components family delegates through the small [`component-pages.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/component-pages.ts) runner to [`component-discovery.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/component-discovery.ts) and [`component-detail.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/component-detail.ts). Their unit suites are [`catalogue_component_discovery_test.ts`](../../tests/catalogue_component_discovery_test.ts) and [`catalogue_component_detail_test.ts`](../../tests/catalogue_component_detail_test.ts). Compare has its own [`compare.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/compare.ts) runner and existing [`catalogue_compare_test.ts`](../../tests/catalogue_compare_test.ts). Shared CLI-host checks live in [`cli-preview.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/cli-preview.ts), called by [`terminal.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/terminal.ts).

The public Component population runner, [`components.ts`](../../scripts/conformance/catalogue/components.ts), is enrolled separately as `component-contracts`. Its metadata-driven scenario, accessibility, axis, status, and focus checks remain independent of route-page ownership. [`catalogue_components_test.ts`](../../tests/catalogue_components_test.ts) retains public example/panel assertions. Shared fixture loading and semantic-copy assertions live in `component-conformance-page.ts` and `metadata-copy.ts`; route owners call those helpers and own their own journeys.
