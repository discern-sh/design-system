# Cards, navigation, and identity at local width

Content-bearing structures spend vertical space to preserve meaning. [Card](../../src/components/display/card/) remains a passive surface: place a heading and essential content first, quiet metadata after them, then a specifically named native action. Multiple actions are siblings, never links nested inside a full-card action. Nested Cards retain the shared flat treatment. The crowded canonical example demonstrates that composition without imposing a new slot API on consumers.

[Tabs](../../src/components/navigation/tabs/) wraps its strip and long labels. It retains its existing tab/panel contract and DOM-order Left/Right, Home/End navigation across rows; it does not become a two-dimensional keyboard grid. Each label starts on the panel's content edge. Every tab carries its own hairline baseline, extended across the gap to the next label and continued to the strip's end after the last tab, so a wrapped strip draws one divider per row and the selected indicator always sits on a baseline instead of floating mid-stack. A tab's hover wash, press wash and keyboard focus ring share one rounded pill that bleeds `--discern-space-2` past the label and stays clear of the baseline; that pill is also the tab's pointer target, so the gap between labels activates nothing. Hover and press are neutral ink washes, never accent, so neither outranks the selected indicator in dark or under an Accent scope; hover applies only to pointers that can hover. An unavailable tab steps its ink down to the Segmented control's unavailable ink. The panel keeps its gap outside its own box and draws its focus ring `--discern-space-2` out, matching the pill. The pill and that ring hang into the gutter, so a clipping container needs at least that much inline padding to show them whole. Forced colours keep the foundation ring around the label alone and thicken the indicator to outweigh the baseline. [`core_navigation_polish_test.tsx`](../../tests/core_navigation_polish_test.tsx) guards that alignment, the per-row baseline, the pointer target, the neutral washes and the forced-colours indicator. [Breadcrumbs](../../src/components/navigation/breadcrumbs/) wraps ordered ancestors and the complete current location, keeping separators attached to their ancestor. Every crumb line shares one 1.5rem pitch, and each link carries a little inline padding so its focus ring clears the text and the separators; focus changes ink without the hover underline. Neither needs scrolling or a collapsed-item disclosure to reveal current state.

The [People group](../../src/components/people/) keeps readable names through local wrapping. Avatar is the shared image authority: its decorative monogram remains beneath an optional empty-alt image, while the named outer image role or adjacent visible name owns the meaningful alternative. A failed or loading image therefore retains initials in static HTML, without a client event handler. Consumer-provided custom avatar slots retain responsibility for their own fallback and alternatives. AvatarGroup wraps its explicit population and expands the last count to fit; `max` is a content decision rather than a viewport-dependent hidden count. Its overlap stays at a fifth of the avatar size, each overlapped monogram centres in the crescent the next disc leaves visible, and every grouped identity is opaque: photo avatars share the monogram fill, so a loading or failed photo never shows the overlapped disc, and a failed photo repaints its initials over the browser's broken-image glyph.

Identity badges share one grammar. Avatar presence and Agent avatar status sit on an opaque face inside a cut-out ring, and every identity cut-out (the AvatarGroup disc ring, the presence ring and the status ring) reads one role, `--discern-avatar-ring`, which defaults to the surface; a consumer placing identities on the canvas sets it once and every cut-out follows. Both badges share a `0.5rem` floor; presence at `xs` steps outward so it never covers the monogram. Shape carries state without colour, because the default monochrome theme renders every tone as grey and forced colours strip tones altogether: presence is solid when online, a crescent when away, barred when busy and hollow when offline; agent status is a centred dot when working, half-filled when waiting, barred when blocked, checked when done and hollow when idle. Every agent mark sits on the badge face, so each contrasts with the inverse tile in both themes, and blocked shares the warning tone Workflow uses for work that needs a person. The marks are drawn with borders rather than gradients or type, so they keep distinct shapes in forced colours; the working pulse fades only the dot, never the face or ring, and the status word stays in the tile's accessible label. Mention and Agent mention are one inline chip that wraps across lines like highlighted text (its fragments stay inside the line, so wrapping never overflows the column), so a trailing full stop or a Byline lede never wraps alone and a chip avatar stays beside the first word of the name. A static chip is a quiet inset well in ink; only a linked chip takes the Accent tint ladder (100 at rest, 200 on hover, 300 on press) with transitions, and its foundation focus ring sits on the chip's own edge so it never touches neighbouring words. Only the shape (rounded for people, `radius-sm` for agents) and the sigil differ. Persona, Agent persona and both mentions keep their avatar or sigil beside the first line of a wrapped name, while a one-line name stays centred on its avatar; an Agent persona status label keeps sentence case. Byline connector words (the lede and any plain text between authors) share the muted meta tone and flow with the names, so names carry emphasis through their own chips. Profile card names break at their own hyphens rather than inside a word, and the card's padding narrows with its allocation. [Identity and operations geometry tests](../../tests/identity_operations_geometry_test.tsx) guard monogram clearance, opaque grouped identities including failed photos, the shared ring role, badge opacity, forced-colour shape distinctness, the `xs` presence clearance, inline chip flow and focus, and first-line alignment.

Start review with each affected Component's `narrow-content` posture in the [existing review instrument](../60-catalogue/visual-review.md). [Content structure tests](../../tests/content_structure_polish_test.tsx) exercise 240, 390, and 720 CSS pixel local widths at normal and doubled root text, including unbroken metadata, long names, the last selected tab, failed photos, and a three-digit overflow count. This is an allocated-family regression guard, not a claim about arbitrary consumer children or other Component groups. Existing CLI identity and attribution exact-frame tests preserve their terminal contracts.
