## Background

The diagram programme landed a complete typed-spec → validate/layout → closed-scene → independent-projections system, and wave 1A just generalized its codegen into a kind-family framework and promoted the family-neutral authorities into shared internal modules. What does not exist anywhere in the repository is numeric machinery: no scale or tick mathematics, no deterministic number/unit formatter, no series data model, no sub-cell glyph ramps, no categorical series palette. Charts are a sibling kind family — quantities on scales, where diagrams are identity and topology — because the landed `DiagramScene` grammar is structurally wrong for marks: connectors require arrowheads (`src/diagram/scene.ts:133`), text is middle-anchored with exactly three placements (`scene.ts:83`), the paint set is a closed 13-member semantic vocabulary (`scene.ts:20-33`), and `conformDiagramScene` actively forbids chart-typical geometry such as adjacent bars and gridlines passing under marks.

This wave builds the chart foundation entirely internally, for the same reason diagram wave 1A stayed internal: publishing a Component now would either violate the fixed Component anatomy or make a temporary CLI exemption public history. Everything here is package-private groundwork that 3A ships: the boundary ADR, the numeric and glyph authorities, the palette feasibility spike with its permanent guards, the `ChartScene` grammar with its own conformance proofs, and the `bar` kind's neutral core enrolled through the family framework.

## Fixed chart-foundation contracts

The programme README's fixed-contracts table binds this wave; these restate the rows it must honour most directly:

- `ChartSpec` data is recursively readonly and JSON-safe, with a mandatory non-empty accessible title and summary. Labels are plain text; series and category identifiers are stable printable ASCII.
- The pipeline is `validate → kind layout → ChartScene → conformance`; no projection ever receives an unproven scene. `describeChart` dispatches through the same generated kind authority.
- Equal spec and options produce byte-identical scenes, descriptions, tick sets, and formatted numbers. Tick generation and label formatting run in scaled-decimal integer space; rounding is half-away-from-zero everywhere; locale and clock APIs are banned from the chart graph by a permanent guard.
- Honesty rules are validation and grammar facts, not conventions: zero baseline for length-encoding kinds, bins declared in the spec, no resampling, no dual axes, no min-relative scaling.
- Kind enrolment is generated. A chart kind declares metadata, budgets with chart-appropriate remedies, CLI stance, and honesty tier at birth, and the family codegen fails when a required surface is missing.
- Wave 1A's shared modules are consumed, never re-implemented. A gap found in a promoted authority is fixed at the authority, with the diagram suite proving nothing regressed.
- This wave adds no dependency, no public export, and no `tokens.ts` change.
