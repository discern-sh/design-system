## Background

Bar proved the architecture: typed spec, validated layout into a conformed `ChartScene`, deterministic SVG, a description-first accessibility authority whose data table is the universal lossless terminal fallback, and an enhanced CLI frame built on the annotated-glyph camp—label plus eighth-block-scaled geometry plus printed value. The five remaining kinds each earn their place by a distinct reader verb recorded with real corpus evidence in the taxonomy note: follow a quantity over an ordered domain (`line`), see how recorded values spread (`distribution`), read one quantity across a grid of two category sets (`heatmap`), judge the relationship between two quantities (`scatter`), and compare before → after per item (`slope`). Honesty tiers are fixed by the programme README: `distribution` and `slope` are **exact** (every authored value appears printed in the frame), `line`, `heatmap`, and `scatter` are **faithful** (declared-resolution approximation that prints extremes, states its resolution, and never distorts, with the lossless table one mode away). An enhanced frame that exceeds its declared tier is a review defect, not a feature.

Sparkline is deliberately not a kind: it has no axes, no scale vocabulary, and is lossy by design, so it ships as a display Component whose metadata records that posture, exactly as Meter stayed a component rather than a gauge chart. A request for pie, radar, gauges, bubbles, dual axes, stacked area, 3D, or streaming remains refused with a named remedy in generated author guidance rather than becoming a kind.

### `line` — enhanced, faithful

- Follows one or more series over an ordered-numeric or date x-domain through the shared scale authority; optional area rendering is a single-series fill variant of line, never a stacked area. Explicit nulls render as declared gaps distinct from zero.
- CLI draws box-drawing glyphs at Unicode and asterisk-with-dot interpolation at ASCII, one authored point per column with no resampling ever—when cardinality exceeds the width envelope the frame declines with a typed reason rather than decimating. Extremes print exactly (corner-annotation style); mono output limits series to the per-glyph budget because crossing colourless lines are ambiguous, and declines beyond it.

### `distribution` — enhanced, exact

- Shows how recorded values spread: a histogram whose bin edges are author-declared in the spec or produced by a pinned deterministic rule named there—never renderer-adaptive—plus a box five-number-summary variant.
- CLI is the terminal-native horizontal form: bin-range label, eighth-block bar, and the printed count on every row, so the frame is genuinely lossless within its width envelope. The box variant draws the box-drawing summary row with all five numbers printed beneath it—the annotation is the lossless layer.

### `heatmap` — enhanced, faithful

- Reads one quantity across a grid of two category sets. Bins are declared in the spec; the legend states the bin edges on every surface. SVG encodes magnitude on the sequential accent ramp (lightness carries the value).
- CLI renders the shade ramp at Unicode and digit-bin cells at ASCII—the digit form is the more honest one, preserving the exact bin index per cell. A scale needing finer distinction than the ramp honestly carries re-bins with declared edges or declines; it never dithers silently.

### `scatter` — enhanced, faithful, conservative envelope

- Judges the relationship between two quantities: per-series marker glyphs for at most three series, each series slot wearing its full paired bundle so colour is never the only distinction.
- CLI quantizes points to the marker grid; collisions render as a count digit, and any cell whose count exceeds nine triggers a typed decline rather than a lie. Axis extremes annotate the corners. The exact pairs always live in the description table—this is the kind where the quantized picture and the exact table matter most as a pair.

### `slope` — enhanced, exact

- Compares before → after per item: SVG is a slopegraph with two ordinal axes, connecting lines, and direct labels rather than a legend.
- CLI is not a graphic at all: the rendered enhanced frame is a delta list built on the triangle authority—label, both authored values, direction triangle, and the signed delta—which prints every authored value and is arguably clearer than the drawing. This textual frame is the kind's permanent terminal form, declared in Metadata, not a decline.
